# Founding Cohort Launch Checklist

## Legal and ownership

- [ ] Independent Cost Per Lease legal entity formed or designated.
- [ ] Entity owns research IP, marks, agreements, and retained aggregates.
- [ ] Data contribution agreement reviewed and approved by counsel.
- [ ] Privacy, incident, backup, and destruction terms reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Trademark clearance completed for Cost Per Lease and proposed marks.
- [ ] Certification mark explicitly deferred.

## Research readiness

- [ ] External multifamily operator reviewer appointed.
- [ ] External finance/accounting reviewer appointed.
- [ ] CPL Standard v0.1 definitions approved for pilot.
- [ ] Calculation specification and quality controls approved.
- [ ] Synthetic fixture independently reproduced twice.
- [ ] Controlled `.xlsx` workbook generated and visually verified.
- [ ] No definition or input schema changes after pilot intake freezes.

## Security readiness

- [ ] Encrypted file-transfer/data-room provider selected.
- [ ] Provider, region, subprocessors, retention, versioning, and backup expiry documented.
- [ ] Multi-factor authentication enforced.
- [ ] Named least-privilege access provisioned.
- [ ] Access logging tested.
- [ ] Identity mapping stored separately.
- [ ] Deletion from active storage, trash, versions, and local devices tested.
- [ ] Incident contact and stop-work process tested.

## Recruitment readiness

- [ ] 25–30 candidate firms identified.
- [ ] Invitation and objection responses approved.
- [ ] Fit-call calendar and owner assigned.
- [ ] Qualification checklist ready.
- [ ] Target mix is approximately 10 firms and 50 eligible properties.
- [ ] Two pilot firms selected before general intake.

## Publication readiness

- [ ] At least five firms and 25 valid properties overall.
- [ ] Every reported cell passes five-firm, ten-property, and 25-percent concentration rules.
- [ ] All public property observations contain four valid TTM quarters.
- [ ] Owner-balanced sensitivity completed.
- [ ] Destruction certificates complete.
- [ ] Legal and methodology review complete.
- [ ] Limitations, corrections channel, and version history included.
- [ ] If any gate fails, output labeled Methodology Pilot rather than Benchmark.
