## Methodology

- Population: stabilized, conventional, market-rate multifamily.
- Observation: property-quarter; primary benchmark uses property TTM results.
- Statistics: count, median, P25, P75; equal property weighting plus an owner-
  balanced sensitivity check.
- Quality: only valid, reconciled observations included.
- Privacy: no participant or property records retained after assessment; qualifying
  aggregates only.
- Separation: participant data was not available to Leaser AI or used commercially.
