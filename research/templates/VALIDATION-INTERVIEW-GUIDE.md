# Cohort Validation Interview Guide

**Length:** 60 minutes
**Purpose:** Validate definitions, source mappings, allocations, and anomalies without
collecting additional prohibited data.

## 1. Opening (5 minutes)

- Confirm participant code, eligible properties, and reporting quarters.
- Reconfirm that the submitted file contains property-level aggregates only.
- Explain that factual mappings can be corrected; methodology is controlled by
  Cost Per Lease and changes are versioned.

## 2. Lease events and occupancy (10 minutes)

- How does the source system distinguish executed leases from applications and
  scheduled move-ins?
- Are transfers, employee units, corporate holds, and cancellations excluded?
- How are renewals distinguished from month-to-month continuations?
- What explains any quarter with leases, renewals, move-outs, or occupancy outside
  its normal range?

## 3. Marketing and attribution (10 minutes)

- Which ledger and platform sources produce each media category?
- Are ILS contracts allocated directly to the property?
- What enters marketing agency, technology, labor, and event/referral categories?
- What attribution method produced attributed lease counts?
- Is source coverage incomplete or changed during the history?

## 4. Leasing and renewal operations (10 minutes)

- How are property and centralized labor split between new leasing and renewals?
- What driver allocates centralized labor and technology?
- Are commissions recorded at execution, move-in, or payment?
- Are renewal communications and incentives separated from acquisition?

## 5. Concessions, vacancy, and turnover (10 minutes)

- Does the concession value represent face value or accounting amortization?
- What GL definition is used for vacancy loss?
- Does vacancy include bad debt, delinquency, employee units, or model units?
- Which turnover/make-ready costs are expensed, and are capital projects excluded?

## 6. Financial context and reconciliation (10 minutes)

- Are revenue and NOI accrual, cash, or mixed basis?
- Which reports or ledger totals support concessions, vacancy, turnover, revenue,
  and NOI?
- Explain every reconciliation difference beyond the tolerance.
- Did accounting classifications or allocation methods change during the history?

## 7. Close (5 minutes)

- Read back mapping and allocation decisions.
- Identify unresolved items, owner, and deadline.
- Confirm report timing, correction window, and destruction clock.
- Ask what decision the participant expects the assessment to clarify.

## Interview record

Record decisions in the restricted reclassification log. Do not place confidential
values, property names, or source screenshots in the administrative tracker.
