# Founding CPL Cohort Recruitment Kit

## 1. Ideal participant

An owner or operator with 3–8 stabilized, conventional, market-rate multifamily
properties; at least four quarters of property-level operating data; and a finance,
marketing, or operations leader willing to validate definitions.

## 2. Invitation email

**Subject:** Help define the first standard for the economics of occupancy

Cost Per Lease is forming a private founding cohort of 10 multifamily owners and
operators to test a shared method for measuring what new leases, renewals, and
occupied units actually cost.

Participants contribute approved property-quarter aggregates for approximately
five stabilized properties and receive a confidential assessment showing Media,
Acquisition, Fully Loaded, and Economic New-Lease CPL; Renewal CPL; Cost Per
Occupied Unit; cost composition; data confidence; and leave-one-participant-out
peer ranges where the sample permits.

We do not request resident, applicant, unit-level, or raw CRM data. Participant-
and property-level data is never shared, sold, supplied to Leaser AI, or used to
train AI. Restricted source and normalized files are destroyed after the assessment;
only disclosure-safe aggregate statistics may be retained for the public methodology
and benchmark brief.

There is no fee for the founding cohort. The exchange is approved data and review
time for a private assessment and a voice in testing CPL Standard v0.1.

Would you be open to a 30-minute fit conversation?

Amit Rathore
Founder, Cost Per Lease

## 3. Fit-call agenda

1. Current decisions that occupancy-cost reporting does not answer.
2. Eligible property population and available quarter history.
3. Existing marketing, leasing, renewal, concession, vacancy, and turnover data.
4. Authority to contribute approved aggregates.
5. Privacy, separation, destruction, and aggregate-use terms.
6. Named data owner and finance reconciliation contact.
7. Timeline and expected private assessment.

## 4. Qualification questions

- Which specific operating or capital-allocation decision would a true CPL change?
- How do you calculate cost per lease today, and who prepares it?
- Can you provide 3–8 stabilized properties and eight quarters where available?
- Can costs be allocated at property level without resident or unit records?
- Who can reconcile concessions, vacancy loss, turnover, revenue, and NOI?
- Who has authority to approve this data contribution?
- Would a confidential assessment be valuable without public recognition?
- Would you repeat the assessment if it changed or clarified a decision?

## 5. One-paragraph cohort description

The Founding CPL Benchmark Cohort is a private research program for 10 multifamily
owners and operators. It tests CPL Standard v0.1 across approximately 50 stabilized
properties and provides each participant a confidential assessment. Cost Per Lease
retains no participant- or property-level operating data after the assessment and
publishes only aggregate findings that meet strict sample and concentration rules.

## 6. Objection responses

### “We cannot share resident data.”

Good. The cohort prohibits resident, applicant, employee, unit-level, contact, and
raw CRM data. It uses property-quarter financial and operating aggregates only.

### “We already calculate marketing CPL.”

Media CPL is the first layer. The assessment separates demand generation, leasing
labor and technology, concessions, vacancy and turnover, renewals, and total cost
per occupied unit.

### “Is this a Leaser AI sales exercise?”

No. The cohort is owned and operated independently. Data and private results are
not available to Leaser AI, used for prospecting, or conditioned on a commercial
conversation.

### “Will our data be published?”

No participant or property record is published or shared. Only cohort statistics
meeting the minimum of five firms, ten properties, and no more than 25 percent from
one firm may be retained or released.

### “What happens to our files?”

Restricted source, normalized, working, and report files are destroyed within 30
days after report acceptance, subject to the disclosed immutable-backup lifecycle.
You receive a destruction certificate.

## 7. Commitment request

The participant commits to one intake orientation, one approved submission, one
validation interview, one private readout, and a five-business-day factual correction
window. Cost Per Lease commits to the assessment, controls, separation, deletion,
and destruction confirmation described in the approved agreement.
