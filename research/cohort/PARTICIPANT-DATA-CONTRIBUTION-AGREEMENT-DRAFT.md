## 1. Purpose

Participant will provide approved, aggregate property-quarter operating data so
CPL may prepare confidential assessments and, subject to the safeguards below,
produce non-identifying aggregate research about occupancy economics.

## 2. Approved data

Participant may submit only the fields in the cohort data dictionary for eligible
properties. Participant will not submit resident, applicant, employee, unit-level,
contact, demographic, protected-class, credit, screening, payment-account,
government-identifier, or raw CRM data.

Participant represents that it is authorized to provide the approved data for the
purposes in this Agreement and that submission does not violate another agreement.

## 3. Limited license

Participant retains ownership of submitted data. Participant grants CPL a limited,
non-exclusive, non-transferable license during the term to:

1. validate and normalize approved data;
2. calculate the measures in CPL Standard v0.1;
3. prepare Participant's confidential assessment; and
4. create and retain aggregate statistics that satisfy Section 6.

No other right is granted. CPL may not sell, license, commercialize, or use the
submitted data for advertising, sales prospecting, product training, model training,
or a participant ranking.

## 4. Separation from Leaser AI

CPL operates this research independently. CPL will not provide submitted data,
property-level derived values, identity mappings, or confidential assessments to
Leaser AI or use them to qualify commercial prospects. Any separate commercial use
requires a new, explicit written authorization that is not a condition of cohort
participation.

## 5. Confidential assessment

CPL will provide Participant a private assessment using its approved data and a
leave-one-participant-out peer set where sample thresholds permit. Participant may
correct factual mapping errors within five business days after delivery. CPL retains
editorial and methodological control over definitions and peer statistics.

Participant will treat non-public cohort ranges and methodology drafts as CPL
confidential information until CPL publishes them.

## 6. Aggregate research

CPL may retain and publish only statistics that:

- contain at least five unrelated firms and ten properties per reported cell;
- contain no more than 25 percent contribution from any firm;
- do not contain identifying labels or free text; and
- cannot reasonably identify Participant through cell differencing or context.

CPL will not name Participant publicly without separate written permission.

## 7. Security and access

CPL will use the controls in the attached Data Handling and Destruction Protocol,
including encrypted transfer, multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access,
separate identity mappings, access logging, and prohibition on submission to AI
systems or ordinary email.

Approved subprocessors and their backup behavior must be listed in an exhibit before
data transfer. CPL remains responsible for their use within the agreed purpose.

## 8. Retention and destruction

The correction window ends when Participant accepts the report or five business
days after delivery if no correction is submitted. Within 30 calendar days after
that date, CPL will delete restricted source data, normalized records, identity
mappings, working calculations, internal report copies, and recoverable working
copies, subject to disclosed immutable-backup expiry.

CPL may retain executed agreements, non-value administrative logs, destruction
records, methodology, and qualifying aggregate research. CPL will provide a signed
destruction certificate.

## 9. Withdrawal and corrections

Participant may withdraw before its data is incorporated into an approved aggregate
table. CPL will stop processing and destroy its restricted data under Section 8.
After qualifying aggregates have been created and source records destroyed, those
non-identifying aggregates cannot be removed or reconstructed by participant.

## 10. Incidents

CPL will investigate suspected unauthorized access, contain affected systems, and
notify Participant without unreasonable delay when required by this Agreement or
applicable law. The parties will cooperate on legally required notices.

## 11. Intellectual property

Participant owns its submitted data. CPL owns the CPL Standard, definitions,
classification system, templates, validation rules, assessment format, aggregate
compilations, and research expression, excluding Participant's submitted facts.
Feedback may be used to improve the standard without identifying Participant.

## 12. No warranties; limitations

The assessment is research and management information, not accounting, legal, tax,
investment, valuation, fair-housing, or causal-attribution advice. Counsel must add
appropriate warranty disclaimers, liability limits, indemnities, remedies, insurance
requirements, and exclusions consistent with applicable law and the parties' risk.

## 13. Term and termination

The Agreement begins on the effective date and ends after CPL completes the assessment,
destruction, and any permitted aggregate research. Confidentiality, ownership,
aggregate-use, security, and destruction obligations survive as appropriate.
